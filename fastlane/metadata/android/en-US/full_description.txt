<p>LibreCrate is a private, offline vault for your documents. Store PDFs, ebooks, boarding passes and wallet cards, comics, photos, and notes — all safely locked away and organized.</p>

<p>Everything you save is encrypted on your device and protected by a password you set. Nobody can read your files without you. LibreCrate never connects to the internet, so your documents never leave your phone.</p>

<p>Features:</p>
<ul>
<li>Store PDFs, EPUBs, Apple Wallet passes, CBZ comics, images, and notes</li>
<li>Everything stays encrypted on your device, protected by your password</li>
<li>Built-in PDF reader with pinch-to-zoom and smooth scrolling</li>
<li>Search your library, including the text inside documents</li>
<li>Sort and filter to keep your library tidy</li>
<li>Add files easily — share to LibreCrate or pick them from your phone</li>
<li>Back up and restore your vault as one encrypted file</li>
<li>Use on multiple devices — restore your backup on another phone, tablet, or computer</li>
<li>Fully offline — no account, no tracking, no ads</li>
</ul>
